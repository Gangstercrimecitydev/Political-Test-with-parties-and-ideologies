# 🧭 Interactive Political Compass (v1.0)

An interactive, web-based political compass designed to help users map their political alignment across economic and social axes and compare their results with international political parties and EU parliamentary groups.

> [!WARNING]
> **Important Disclaimer:**
> - **No Voting Recommendation:** This tool is strictly for educational and entertainment purposes. It does **not** provide voting advice or political endorsements.
> - **Accuracy:** Results are approximations based on generalized political axes and may not fully reflect complex real-world positions.
> - **Under Active Development:** This project is currently work-in-progress. Certain features, data points, or calculations may be incomplete, unverified, or subject to change in future updates.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- **24 Detailed Statements:** Carefully split into two balanced categories:
  - 📈 **12 Questions on Economy, Finance & Labor**
  - 🏛️ **12 Questions on Society, Culture & State Governance**
- **Interactive Ideology Overlay:** Toggleable political ideology zones (e.g., Social Democracy, Neoliberalism, Left Liberalism) directly on the compass.
- **Dynamic Party Comparison:** Compare your red position dot against political landscapes around the globe.
- **Privacy First:** Runs 100% in your browser — no personal data or quiz results are stored or transmitted.

---

## 🌍 Included Countries & Frameworks

The compass currently supports position overlays for:
- **34 Individual Countries** (e.g., Germany, USA, UK, Switzerland, France, Poland, etc.)
- **EU Parliamentary Groups Overview** (e.g., EPP, S&D, Renew, ECR, PfE, Greens/EFA, ESN, The Left)
- **National Parties within EU Frameworks**
